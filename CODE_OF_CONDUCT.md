# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Resume Saathi is committed to being a welcoming and respectful space for everyone.
All contributors and maintainers are expected to behave professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Assume positive intent and communicate clearly.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the code.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful language.
- Personal attacks, threats, or intimidation.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- Repository discussions, issues, and pull requests.
- Any public or private communication related to this project.

## Reporting

If you observe or experience unacceptable behavior, report it to the maintainers:

- Open a private security-style report as described in SECURITY.md.
- Or contact the repository owner directly through a private channel.

All reports are reviewed fairly and handled confidentially whenever possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation for violations of this Code of Conduct.
